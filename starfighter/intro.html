MISSION BRIEFING
Classification: Combat — All Pilots
YEAR 2387
Situation
Earth Orbit. Last Defense Line.

Humanity spans the solar system, but Earth remains home. The UEDF Battleship Resolute — our last heavy carrier — holds the orbital defense perimeter. If the Resolute falls, the line collapses.

Threat Assessment
HIVE SIGMA

An alien force from deep space. Organic-metallic hybrid ships — grown, not built. They don't communicate. They don't negotiate. They consume. They attack in waves. They learn. They adapt. They don't stop. Earth is their next target.

Your Orders
DEFEND THE RESOLUTE
  • Launch from the Resolute's hangar bay
  • Engage and destroy all enemy fighters each wave
  • Prioritize alien motherships — they attack the Resolute directly
  • Return between waves for repairs and rearming
  • There is no final wave. There is only how long you hold the line.
